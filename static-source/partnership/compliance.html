Traceability
Traceability

Batch-level traceability from forest to shelf 从森林到货架的批次级可追溯

Every batch of HushKhan pine nuts is traceable to a specific harvest region and processing date. We keep samples from each production lot for quality verification and customer reference. 每一批HushKhan松子均可追溯至特定采摘区域与加工日期。我们保留每批生产样品，用于质量验证和客户参考。

  • Harvest region GPS coordinates recorded 记录采摘区域GPS坐标
  • Processing date and batch number on every package 每包均标注加工日期与批次号
  • Third-party lab tests per export shipment 每批出口货物均经第三方实验室检测
  • Temperature and humidity logs during storage 储存期间记录温湿度

Laboratory test reports 实验室检测报告

Nutritional analysis, microbiological safety, heavy metal screening, and aflatoxin test results available per shipment. 每批货物均可提供营养分析、微生物安全、重金属筛查及黄曲霉毒素检测结果。

Tests

What we test for every export lot 每批出口货物检测项目

Reports are prepared by accredited third-party laboratories and shared with buyers before shipment. We can also customize tests based on importer country requirements. 报告由认可的第三方实验室出具，并在发货前与买家共享。我们也可根据进口国要求定制检测项目。

  • Microbiological safety (TPC, E. coli, Salmonella) 微生物安全（菌落总数、大肠杆菌、沙门氏菌）
  • Heavy metals (Pb, Cd, As) 重金属（铅、镉、砷）
  • Aflatoxin B1 & total aflatoxin 黄曲霉毒素B1及总黄曲霉毒素
  • Moisture, fat, protein profiles 水分、脂肪、蛋白质指标
